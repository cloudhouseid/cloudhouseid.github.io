## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Personal Identifiable Information (PII)
**Our Applications do NOT collect, require, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).**
* No user account registration or sign-in is required to use or play our Applications.
* We do not collect your real name, physical address, email address, phone number, contacts list, or government identification.
* We do not access or collect your device microphone, camera, photo library, or personal files.

### B. Local Application & Gameplay Data (On-Device Storage)
To provide a smooth, responsive, and uninterrupted user experience, our Applications store user progress, settings, and preferences strictly **locally on your device** (utilizing local on-device databases and storage mechanisms such as Android Jetpack DataStore and Room database). This local data may include:
* Scores, achievements, level progression, and gameplay statistics
* Unlocked in-game content, catalog items, and theme selections
* User settings (such as audio volume, vibration haptics, and display preferences)

This data is stored strictly on your local device storage and is **never transmitted to external developer servers**.

### C. Technical and Device Information Collected Automatically
When using our Applications, integrated third-party SDKs (such as Google Mobile Ads / AdMob) may automatically process non-sensitive device and diagnostic information to ensure application functionality, stability, and advertisement delivery:
* Device model, manufacturer, and screen resolution
* Operating system version (Android version)
* Internet Protocol (IP) address (used for approximate geographic region and localized ad delivery)
* Google Advertising ID (`AD_ID` / GAID)
* Network connection type (Wi-Fi or Mobile)
* Non-identifying crash and performance diagnostics

## 5. Advertising and Opt-Out Rights

Our free Applications are funded through mobile advertisements provided by Google AdMob. You have full control over personalized advertising on Android devices:

### How to Reset or Delete Your Advertising ID:
1. Open your Android device **Settings**.
2. Go to **Google** > **Ads** (or **Privacy** > **Ads**).
3. Tap **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
4. You can also toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization** or enable Privacy Sandbox features where available.
