## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Personal Identifiable Information (PII)
**Piano Tiles does NOT collect, require, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).**
* No user account registration or sign-in is required to play.
* We do not collect your real name, physical address, email address, phone number, contacts list, or government identification.
* We do not access or collect your device microphone, camera, photo library, or personal files.

### B. Local Gameplay Data (On-Device Storage)
To provide an optimal rhythm gaming experience, **Piano Tiles** stores user gameplay progress locally on your device (utilizing Android Jetpack DataStore and Room database). This data includes:
* High scores, star ratings, and game statistics
* Song unlocks, custom tile themes, and difficulty progress
* Game settings (sound volume, vibration toggle, and display preferences)

This gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device storage and is **never transferred to external developer servers**.

### C. Technical and Device Information Collected Automatically
When using the application, integrated third-party SDKs (such as Google Mobile Ads / AdMob) may automatically process non-sensitive device and diagnostic information:
* Device model, manufacturer, and screen resolution
* Operating system version (Android version)
* Internet Protocol (IP) address (for general location and region-appropriate ad delivery)
* Google Advertising ID (`AD_ID` / GAID)
* Network connection type (Wi-Fi or Mobile)
* Non-identifying crash and performance diagnostics

## 4. Device Permissions Requested

**Piano Tiles** declares and utilizes only the minimum required permissions to function properly:

| Permission | Technical Identifier | Purpose & Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Internet Access** | `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required to fetch remote music catalogs and display ads via Google AdMob. |
| **Network State** | `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` & `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE` | Allows the app to verify network connectivity before requesting ads or streaming music data. |
| **Vibration** | `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Provides haptic feedback when tapping piano tiles (can be disabled in game settings). |
| **Advertising ID** | `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` | Standard Android permission (Android 13+) used by Google AdMob to access the advertising identifier. |
| **Notifications** | `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Optional permission on Android 13+ to send gameplay updates and daily challenge reminders (can be disabled anytime). |

## 5. Advertising and Opt-Out Rights

**Piano Tiles** is funded through mobile advertisements provided by Google AdMob. You have full control over personalized advertising on Android devices:

### How to Reset or Delete Your Advertising ID:
1. Open your Android device **Settings**.
2. Go to **Google** > **Ads** (or **Privacy** > **Ads**).
3. Tap **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
4. You can also toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization** or enable Privacy Sandbox features where available.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion (User Rights)

We respect your privacy rights and give you complete control over your data:

* **Local Data Deletion:** Because all your game progress, settings, and high scores are stored locally on your device, you can permanently erase all data at any time by:
  1. Opening device **Settings** > **Apps** > **Piano Tiles**.
  2. Selecting **Storage & Cache** and tapping **Clear Storage** (or **Clear Data**).
  3. Alternatively, uninstalling **Piano Tiles** automatically removes all associated local game data.
* **Advertising Data Opt-Out:** You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID as detailed in Section 5.
* **Privacy Inquiries & Deletion Requests:** You may submit inquiries or request confirmation of data deletion by emailing **faisalamircs@gmail.com**. Requests are handled promptly within 30 days.
